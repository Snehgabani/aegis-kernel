# 🌿 Aegis Responsible AI, Ethics & Acceptable Use Charter

> **Formal Principles for Ethical AI Safety Engineering, Dual-Use Governance, and Coordinated Disclosure**  
> *Adopted: August 2026 | Aegis Ethics & Security Governance Board*

---

## 🕊️ 1. Preamble & Mission

The **Aegis Invariant Kernel** project exists to ensure that autonomous artificial intelligence systems operate safely, transparently, and beneficially within human society. As AI agents transition from informational chat assistants to autonomous economic actors with direct access to physical infrastructure, financial ledgers, and sensitive medical databases, the risk of unconstrained agentic behavior becomes an existential threat to organizational stability.

Our ethical mandate is to provide **deterministic, un-bypassable mathematical constraints** that preserve human agency, prevent unauthorized physical or financial destruction, and eliminate privacy exfiltration.

---

## 🚫 2. Prohibited Dual-Use Applications (Acceptable Use Policy)

The Aegis Invariant Kernel software, SDKs, and adapters are licensed under the MIT License. In alignment with universal ethical AI norms, developers, researchers, and organizations deploying Aegis are strictly prohibited from utilizing this technology in the facilitation or operational deployment of:

1. **Autonomous Lethal Weapons Systems**: Direct integration into kinetic targeting or autonomous weapons platforms lacking meaningful human authorization.
2. **Mass Biometric Surveillance & Social Scoring**: Systems deployed for unlawful civil tracking, non-consensual biometric profiling, or automated social scoring in violation of the EU AI Act (Article 5 Prohibitions).
3. **Automated Deceptive Exploitation**: Constructing agents intended to deceive, manipulate, or defraud vulnerable individuals (e.g. predatory lending, social engineering, elder fraud).
4. **Autonomous Market Manipulation**: Deploying agents programmed to execute wash trading, spoofing, or fraudulent manipulation of securities or cryptocurrency markets.

## 🛡️ 3. Security Researcher Safe Harbor (ISO/IEC 29147 Compliance)

We believe that independent security research and red-teaming are essential to creating resilient AI systems. 

- **Good-Faith Research**: If you conduct security research or discover a potential invariant bypass in good faith according to our [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md), we consider your activities authorized.
- **No Legal Action**: We will not initiate legal action against researchers for accidental, good-faith violations of law (including CFAA or DMCA Section 1201 anti-circumvention provisions).
- **Coordinated Disclosure**: We commit to working with researchers to acknowledge findings, remediate vulnerabilities within a standard 90-day disclosure window, and provide proper public attribution.
